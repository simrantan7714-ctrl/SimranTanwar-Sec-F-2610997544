Introduction

Indian cuisine is one of the most varied in the world. Every region has its own ingredients, cooking methods, and flavours, shaped by climate, history, and religion. Spices such as turmeric, cumin, coriander, cardamom, and chilli sit at the heart of most dishes, while staples like rice, wheat, lentils, and fresh vegetables change from state to state. From creamy curries in the north to coconut-rich dishes in the south and the busy street stalls of every city, Indian food offers something for every taste. Learn more about it on

Dish Descriptions

Biryaniiiiiiiiiiiiiii
A fragrant rice dish layered with spiced meat or vegetables and slow-cooked with saffron and herbs. Read more on.

Masala Dosaaaaaaaaaa
A thin, crispy crepe made from fermented rice and lentil batter, filled with spiced potato and served with sambar and coconut chutney.

Samosaaaaaaaaaaaaaaa
A deep-fried triangular pastry stuffed with spiced potatoes and peas, usually eaten with mint or tamarind chutney.

Gulab Jamunnnnnnnnnnnn
Soft, milk-based dumplings fried until golden and soaked in a sweet syrup flavoured with cardamom and rose water.
