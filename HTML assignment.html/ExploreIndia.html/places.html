Famous Places in India

Six landmarks worth a visit

Taj Mahal, Agra


Built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal, this white marble mausoleum is one of the most recognised buildings in the world. It is known for its perfect symmetry and delicate inlay work.

Hawa Mahal, Jaipur


The "Palace of Winds" is a pink sandstone building with hundreds of small windows. They let royal women watch street festivals unseen while keeping the palace cool.

Golden Temple, Amritsar


Harmandir Sahib is the holiest Gurdwara of Sikhism. Its gold-covered sanctum sits in the middle of a sacred pool, and its community kitchen serves free meals to thousands of visitors every day.

Kerala Backwaters, Alleppey


A network of calm lagoons, canals, and lakes lined with coconut palms. Visitors can travel through it on traditional houseboats and see village life along the water.

Varanasi Ghats


One of the oldest living cities in the world, Varanasi sits on the banks of the Ganges. Its long rows of stone steps, called ghats, are known for morning prayers and the evening Ganga Aarti.

Gateway of India, Mumbai


This basalt arch on the Mumbai waterfront was completed in 1924. It is a popular meeting point for locals and tourists, with views of the Arabian Sea and boats to nearby islands.
