Introduction

India is a land of colour, culture and contrast. From the snow-capped Himalayas in the north to the golden beaches of Goa in the south, every state has its own language, food and traditions. You can walk through ancient forts, relax on peaceful backwaters, explore busy markets and taste some of the best street food in the world. Whether you love history, nature or adventure, India has something special for every traveller.
