College Terminology

Semester
A half-year academic term during which courses are taught and examined.
Credit
A unit that measures the workload and value of a course.
Syllabus
A document outlining the topics and schedule of a course.
Practical
A hands-on lab session where theory is applied.
Attendance
The record of how many classes a student has attended.
Internal Assessment
Marks awarded during the semester through tests, assignments, and participation.
